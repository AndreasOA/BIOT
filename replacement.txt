Figure~\ref{fig:architecture_window_aucpr_macro} provides a visual comparison of \ac{AUCPR} Macro performance across all four architectures and three temporal window configurations. The boxplots show the distribution over five random seeds, with box height representing inter-quartile range (IQR), the horizontal line indicating the median, and whiskers extending to 1.5×IQR. Tight boxes indicate stable performance across seeds, while wide boxes reveal high variability.

\textbf{At 5\,s windows:} \ac{sLSTM} (red) achieves the highest median (0.513) with a narrow IQR, demonstrating both superior performance and training stability. The Linear Transformer (blue) shows comparable median performance (0.468) but substantially wider distribution, indicating higher sensitivity to initialization. \ac{mLSTM} (green) and the combined architecture (purple) cluster around 0.487–0.489 with moderate spread.

\textbf{At 7\,s windows:} The performance hierarchy shifts. \ac{mLSTM} attains the highest median (0.484) and becomes the most stable architecture at this window length. \ac{sLSTM} drops to 0.493 with increased variance, showing wider boxes than at 5\,s. The Linear Transformer maintains 0.465 with moderate spread. The combined model remains at 0.479 with tight distribution.

\textbf{At 9\,s windows:} \ac{sLSTM} recovers to 0.486 median, regaining competitive performance. \ac{mLSTM} degrades substantially to 0.459 with the widest distribution observed across all configurations. The Linear Transformer continues declining to 0.453. The combined architecture maintains remarkable stability at 0.472 with the tightest box, though it never achieves best-in-class median performance.

\textbf{Key observations:} (1)~\ac{sLSTM} exhibits U-shaped performance across windows (strong at 5\,s and 9\,s, weaker at 7\,s); (2)~\ac{mLSTM} shows inverted behavior (peaks at 7\,s, collapses at 9\,s); (3)~The combined architecture provides consistent stability but no performance peaks; (4)~The Linear Transformer degrades monotonically with window length. Detailed architectural interpretations are provided in Section~\ref{sec:window_behaviors}.
